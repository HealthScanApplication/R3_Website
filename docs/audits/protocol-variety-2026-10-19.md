## How variety is built

- **Meals** with a catalogue recipe rotate between published recipes of the same meal slot, carrying every diet flag the original has, plus flags taken from the protocol's name, tags and description (vegan, keto, gluten-free…). Highest health score first, never the same dish twice on one day, and the original comes back each week. Generic "Breakfast"/"Lunch" slots without a recipe get a real `catalog_recipe_id` and take its name (protocolRules rule 4). Drinks and drink-named recipes are never swapped.
- **Exercises** (training categories only: strength, cardio, sports, stretch, gym, calisthenics, HIIT…) rotate between relatives under the same parent activity, or else the same subcategory and muscle group (`muscleGroupOf`). Day 7 and day 14 are rest days, day 4 and day 11 are lighter (60% volume), and week 2 adds one set or 15% more minutes. Beauty, hygiene, wellness and mindfulness activities stay daily.
- **Stays daily:** supplements, hygiene, drinks, sleep anchors, and anything already scheduled by day_number or weekly recurrence.
- **Rules** (rule_do / rule_dont children) are copied with each day copy and re-parented to it. Copies get `task_key || ':d<day>'` to satisfy `protocol_items_protocol_task_key_uq`.
